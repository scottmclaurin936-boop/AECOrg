Draft workflow

Private first. Public by choice.

Future creator workspaces should support private drafts, project files, and an explicit publish step. Sign-in, storage, and editing are not implemented in this static draft.

Planning status: Academy formation, member enrollment, and any nonprofit or tax-exempt status are not represented as completed or approved here.
