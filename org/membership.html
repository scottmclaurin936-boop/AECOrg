Academy / Member access concept

Membership starts with an application.

The founder-selected model is application and review, not automatic registration. This page is a design preview: applications are not open and no accounts can be created.

Not accepting applications: This is a review-only prototype. The steps below are a proposed interface, not an operating Academy policy.
01 / INTEREST

Explore the Academy

Read about the public creative umbrella and its proposed disciplines.

02 / APPLICATION

Complete an application

A future form would gather only founder-approved information, with an appropriate privacy notice.

03 / REVIEW

Wait for a decision

A future authorized reviewer would approve, decline, or request clarification.

04 / ACCESS

Invitation after approval

Account creation would occur only after approval through a secure, separate backend.
