AEC .com / Production-facing concept

Portfolio and production record

A production-facing template for approved work samples, credits, and scope information. No active services or business commitments are claimed.

Unpublished layout: Project cards are placeholders. This is not a booking, quote, or payment page.

Portfolio slots

Each future public entry should have a verified owner, permission to display, source assets, and approved credits.

SLOT 01

Audio production

Approved sample, description, contributors, release status, and rights note.

SLOT 02

Screen production

Approved reel or storyboard preview, project status, and credited roles.

SLOT 03

Design production

Approved images, project context, credits, and publication permissions.

Proposed project record

Minimum metadata

Title, creative owner, approved public summary, medium, role/credits, source provenance, permission status, asset alt text, and public-release approval.

Not implemented

Future intake

No customer form, quote engine, contract, invoice, or payment flow has been built. Those require separate decisions and legal/operational review.
