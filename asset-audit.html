Required fields

Each proposed public asset must identify its source, rights holder, permission record, accessible description, approver, date, intended surface, and checksum.

An entry with every field populated is still not approved until checked against the actual authorization and files. This tool checks structure only, not authenticity or legal rights.

Separation: The magazine has its own future integration decision. AEC .org and AEC .com do not share public assets automatically.
